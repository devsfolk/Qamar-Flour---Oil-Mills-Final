Our Legacy

Three Decades of
Industrial Excellence.

Located in the heart of Lahore Industrial Estate at Plot H-78, Qamar Flour & Oil Mills has stood as a beacon of stability and quality for over 30 years. What started as a humble chakki operation has evolved into a multi-vertical industrial giant.

Our commitment remains unchanged: to process the finest harvest of Pakistan's golden wheat fields and premium oilseeds using zero-waste, high-efficiency technology that preserves every nutrient.

Production Verticals

Division 01

Chakki Gold Flour

Our flour is processed through a unique 12-stage purification system that maintains the natural bran and germ content, delivering the authentic taste of tradition with industrial precision.

  • 100% Whole Wheat
  • Zero Chemical Bleaching
  • Fortified with Zinc & Iron
Division 02

Cold-Pressed Extraction

Utilizing European cold-press technology, we extract oil at temperatures below 45°C to ensure the antioxidants and flavor profiles remain intact. Premium grade, pure essence.

  • Zero Trans Fats
  • High Smoke Point
  • Triple Filtered Purity
